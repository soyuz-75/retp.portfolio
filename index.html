About the artist

« Ce que je sais, c'est que ces œuvres sont le fruit de mon inconscient le plus profond et par conséquent de mon vrai moi. » — RET.P

RET.P is a young French artist driven by visual arts and music. At seventeen, after a difficult period, he began drawing with a marker found in his dorm room — time carved out to escape, pause and breathe. Drawing became a way to heal.

His style sharpened year after year, leading to a first exhibition in 2018 at Le Bon Marché in Paris. Each piece is his flow: the moment of maximum concentration and engagement, when the line runs by itself. The works that emerge are both a picture of how he sees the world and a release from it.
